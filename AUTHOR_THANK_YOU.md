# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Amogh Palasamudram, Jakub Svoboda, Suguman Bansal, and Krishnendu
Chatterjee for developing a direct-learning framework for reachability
specifications and for making its theorem and benchmark structure clear
enough to audit claim by claim.

This repository is intended as a respectful, transparent companion to the
paper. It separates proof certificates, finite calibration, and benchmark
evidence; records the strict-boundary repair in Theorem 3.2; and leaves the
complete QVBS result pending until the required 90-trial evidence exists.
